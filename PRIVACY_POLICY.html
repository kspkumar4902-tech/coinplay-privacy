3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Prevent fraud and maintain security, including detecting bots, emulators, rooted devices, VPN/proxy use, and duplicate accounts through device fingerprinting and risk scoring.
  • Process withdrawal requests, including manual review and verification before any payout.
  • Send notifications about rewards, bonuses, and account activity (you can disable these at any time in your device settings).
  • Improve the App experience, including diagnosing issues, analyzing feature usage, and improving performance.

We do not sell or rent your data, and we do not use your data to profile you for advertising outside the App.

  • Coins are a virtual, in-app currency and have no real or inherent monetary value. They are not legal tender or a financial instrument and cannot be exchanged, sold, or transferred between users.

  • Coins cannot be purchased. We do not sell coins, and there is no way to buy coins or deposit real money into the App. You never pay or wager real money to participate.

5. Withdrawals and Payments

Coin redemption (withdrawal) is a promotional reward feature subject to the following rules and to manual verification:

  • Minimum account age: your account must be at least 20 days old before your first withdrawal.
  • First withdrawal minimum: equivalent of ₹10.
  • Subsequent withdrawals minimum: equivalent of ₹250.
  • Frequency limit: a maximum of 3 withdrawals per calendar month.
  • Manual verification: every withdrawal request is manually reviewed for fraud, eligibility, and policy compliance before approval. Approval and processing are not instant and timing may vary.
  • Payment methods: approved withdrawals are paid via UPI or PayTM. You are responsible for providing accurate payout details.
  • We reserve the right to deny, delay, or reverse any redemption where we detect fraud, abuse, multiple accounts, emulator/VPN use, automated activity, or a violation of our terms. Any applicable taxes on rewards are your responsibility.

7. Data Security

  • Your data is stored using Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) and protected by Firebase Security Rules that default-deny access and restrict each user to reading and writing only their own data.
  • All data is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS / TLS 1.2+) connections.
  • Sign-in uses phone OTP — there is no password to be stolen.
  • Every withdrawal is re-validated server-side in a transaction and manually reviewed before payout.
  • We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the service providers needed to run the App — Google Firebase (authentication, database, notifications), Google AdMob (ads), and an IP-lookup provider (country/fraud signal) — each acting under its own privacy policy.
  • No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we detect a breach affecting your data, we will notify affected users and the appropriate authorities as required by law.
